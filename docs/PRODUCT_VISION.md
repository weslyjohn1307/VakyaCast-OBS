# Product Vision & Live Production Workflows (`PRODUCT_VISION.md`)

## 1. Core Broadcast Use Cases
1. **Instant Reference Badge Presentation**:
   - Operator types `yoh 15:4` or `yoh 15:4-15` in the `/` Quick Reference bar and hits `Enter`.
   - Output displays `యోహాను 15:4` or `యోహాను 15:4-15`.
2. **Whole Chapter Presentation (Zero Verse Picking)**:
   - Pastor announces "Turn to John Chapter 12".
   - Operator types `yoh 12` or clicks Chapter `12` (or presses `C`).
   - Output displays `యోహాను సువార్త 12వ అధ్యాయము` (or `John Chapter 12` in English).
3. **Full-Bible Word & Phonetic Concordance Search (`Ctrl+F`)**:
   - Pastor quotes a phrase mid-sermon without stating the book/chapter.
   - Operator presses `Ctrl+F` and types Telugu (`ప్రేమించెను`, `కాపరి`), English (`abide in me`), or Tanglish (`preminchenu`, `kaapari`, `yehova`).
   - Operator clicks **`Load in Workbench`** or **`▶ Present Ref+Verse`**.
4. **Part-of-Verse Word Highlighting**:
   - Operator clicks key words in the **Interactive Verse Workbench** (e.g., `నాయందు నిలిచియుండుడి`) with `Mode: Highlight Words in Verse`.
   - Full verse appears on screen with those exact words illuminated in an animated Gold/Cyan/Emerald/Crimson broadcast marker.
5. **Present Part-Verse Only (Clause Slicing)**:
   - Long verses (like John 3:16 or John 15:4) can overwhelm a lower-third banner if shown all at once.
   - Operator switches to `Mode: Present Part-Verse Only` and clicks `Part a`, `Part b`, or presses `Alt + ←` / `Alt + →` to step through clauses live on air with larger auto-scaled type and `15:4a` / `15:4b` reference badges.
6. **Mid-Sermon Picture / Missionary Slide-Over**:
   - Pastor mentions a missionary (e.g., *William Carey*) or historical map.
   - Operator copies any image from Google Images and presses `Ctrl+V` anywhere in VakyaCast LT (or searches Wikipedia right inside the `Picture Slide-Over` tab), types the title, and clicks `Show Picture`.
